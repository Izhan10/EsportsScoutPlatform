About PakEsports Scout PRO

Pakistan's first AI-powered esports talent identification platform. We bridge the gap between raw talent and professional opportunities by connecting scouts with the nation's most promising competitive gamers through advanced analytics and video performance review.

🧠

AI Scouting Engine

Our proprietary ESV algorithm evaluates aim, positioning, teamwork, consistency, and decision-making from every uploaded clip to deliver an objective performance score.

🔗

Scout Network

A growing network of certified scouts across Pakistan actively reviewing player footage, building shortlists, and discovering the next generation of esports stars.

📈

Player Development

Players get AI-powered feedback on their gameplay, track their ESV growth over time, and gain visibility among professional scouts and organizations.
